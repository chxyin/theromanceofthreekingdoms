Liu Bei of Zhuo County sighed at the notice and befriended Zhang Fei. The two met Guan Yu in a tavern, and the three, sharing the same ideals, swore brotherhood in the Peach Garden.
liú刘bèi备、guān关yǔ羽、zhāng张fēi飞shuài率lǐng领yì义bīng兵tóu投bēn奔liú刘yān焉，xiān先hòu后jī击bài败huáng黄jīn巾jūn军。sān三rén人yīng英yǒng勇shàn善zhàn战，lǚ屡lì立zhàn战gōng功，míng名shēng声jiàn渐qǐ起。Liu Bei, Guan Yu, and Zhang Fei led their volunteers to join Liu Yan and defeated the Yellow Turban forces in successive battles. The three were brave and skilled in war, repeatedly earning merit, and their reputation gradually grew.

yì议wēn温míng明dǒng董zhuó卓chì叱dīng丁yuán原·kuì馈jīn金zhū珠lǐ李sù肃shuì说lǚ吕bù布At Wenming Garden Dong Zhuo Rebukes Ding Yuan; With Gold and Pearls Li Su Persuades Lü Bu

fèi废hàn汉dì帝chén陈liú留jiàn践wèi位·móu谋dǒng董déi贼mèng孟dé德xiàn献dāo刀Deposing the Han Emperor: Chenliu Takes the Throne; Plotting Against the Traitor Dong: Mengde Presents a Sword

dǒng董zhuó卓xiǎng想shā杀yuán袁shào绍，lǐ李rú儒quàn劝tā他bù不yào要luàn乱shā杀。yuán袁shào绍lí离kāi开luò洛yáng阳，qù去le了jì冀zhōu州。zhōu周bì毖shuō说：“yuán袁shì氏jiā家sì四dài代dōu都yǒu有rén人zuò做dà大guān官，rú如guǒ果bī逼tā他tài太jǐn紧，tā他yī一dìng定huì会fǎn反。bù不rú如gěi给tā他yī一gè个jùn郡shǒu守zuò做，zhè这yàng样tā他jiù就bù不huì会luàn乱lái来。”dǒng董zhuó卓tīng听le了，jiù就ràng让yuán袁shào绍qù去bó渤hǎi海zuò做tài太shǒu守。Dong Zhuo wanted to kill Yuan Shao, but Li Ru advised him not to kill recklessly. It would be better to give him a commandery to govern, and then he will not cause trouble." Dong Zhuo listened and sent Yuan Shao to be the Governor of Bohai.
jiǔ九yuè月chū初yī一，dǒng董zhuó卓qǐng请huáng皇dì帝lái来dào到jiā嘉dé德diàn殿，suǒ所yǒu有de的dà大chén臣dōu都dào到le了。dǒng董zhuó卓bá拔chū出jiàn剑shuō说：“huáng皇dì帝tài太ruò弱le了，bù不néng能guǎn管lǐ理tiān天xià下。jīn今tiān天wǒ我yào要fèi废diào掉tā他，lì立chén陈liú留wáng王wéi为huáng皇dì帝。”dà大chén臣men们dōu都hěn很hài害pà怕，méi没yǒu有rén人gǎn敢shuō说huà话。On the first day of the ninth month, Dong Zhuo summoned the Emperor to the Jiade Hall, and all the ministers were present. Dong Zhuo drew his sword and said: "The Emperor is too weak to rule the empire. Today I will depose him and install the Prince of Chenliu as emperor." The ministers were all terrified and no one dared to speak.
dàn但yǒu有yī一gè个dà大chén臣zhàn站chū出lái来，dà大shēng声shuō说：“dǒng董zhuó卓nǐ你zhè这gè个zéi贼chén臣！nǐ你gǎn敢qī欺piàn骗tiān天xià下，wǒ我yào要yòng用xuě血jiàn溅nǐ你！”tā他jǔ举qǐ起shǒu手lǐ里de的xiàng象jiǎn简jiù就dǎ打dǒng董zhuó卓。dǒng董zhuó卓dà大nù怒，mìng命rén人bǎ把tā他zhuā抓qǐ起lái来。zhè这gè个rén人jiào叫dīng丁guǎn管，shì是shàng尚shū书。dǒng董zhuó卓xià下lìng令bǎ把tā他shā杀le了。dīng丁guǎn管yī一zhí直mà骂dǒng董zhuó卓，dào到sǐ死dōu都bù不pà怕。But one minister stood out and said loudly: "Dong Zhuo, you traitor! You dare to deceive the world—I will splatter you with my blood!" He raised his ivory tablet and struck at Dong Zhuo. Dong Zhuo was furious and ordered him seized. This man was Ding Guan, a Minister. Dong Zhuo ordered him executed. Ding Guan cursed Dong Zhuo continuously and showed no fear even unto death.
dǒng董zhuó卓bǎ把huáng皇dì帝fèi废diào掉le了，ràng让chén陈liú留wáng王dāng当le了huáng皇dì帝。zhè这jiù就shì是hàn汉xiàn献dì帝，dāng当shí时zhǐ只yǒu有jiǔ九suì岁。dǒng董zhuó卓zì自jǐ己dāng当le了xiàng相guó国，quán权lì力fēi非cháng常dà大。tā他bǎ把bèi被fèi废de的huáng皇dì帝hé和tài太hòu后guān关zài在yǒng永ān安gōng宫，bù不zhǔn准tā他men们chū出lái来。Dong Zhuo deposed the Emperor and made the Prince of Chenliu emperor. This was Emperor Xian of Han, who was only nine years old at the time. Dong Zhuo made himself Chancellor and held immense power. He confined the deposed emperor and the empress dowager in the Yong'an Palace and forbade them to leave.
bèi被fèi废de的huáng皇dì帝hěn很nán难guò过，měi每tiān天dōu都kū哭。yǒu有yī一tiān天，tā他kàn看dào到liǎng两zhī只yàn燕zi子zài在yuàn院zi子lǐ里fēi飞，jiù就xiě写le了yī一shǒu首shī诗，shuō说zì自jǐ己hěn很xiǎng想huí回dào到yǐ以qián前de的gōng宫diàn殿。dǒng董zhuó卓zhī知dào道le了zhè这jiàn件shì事，jiù就pài派lǐ李rú儒dài带zhe着dú毒jiǔ酒qù去shā杀tā他。bèi被fèi废de的huáng皇dì帝hé和tài太hòu后dōu都bèi被shā杀le了。The deposed emperor was very sad and cried every day. He went to Wang Yun and borrowed a precious sword, preparing to assassinate Dong Zhuo. The next day, Cao Cao came to Dong Zhuo's residence with the sword. Dong Zhuo was sitting on the bed, with Lü Bu standing beside him.
dǒng董zhuó卓ràng让lǚ吕bù布qù去ná拿yī一pǐ匹hǎo好mǎ马sòng送gěi给cáo曹cāo操。lǚ吕bù布chū出qù去hòu后，cáo曹cāo操xiǎng想dòng动shǒu手。dàn但dǒng董zhuó卓hěn很pàng胖，zuò坐le了yī一huì会r儿jiù就tǎng躺xià下le了，zhuǎn转shēn身cháo朝lǐ里miàn面。cáo曹cāo操jí急máng忙bá拔dāo刀zhǔn准bèi备cì刺shā杀。bù不liào料dǒng董zhuó卓cóng从jìng镜zi子lǐ里kàn看dào到le了，zhuǎn转guò过lái来wèn问：“cáo曹cāo操nǐ你yào要gàn干shén什me么？”Dong Zhuo sent Lü Bu to fetch a good horse to give to Cao Cao. liǎng两gè个rén人yī一qǐ起zǒu走，lù路guò过cáo曹cāo操fù父qīn亲de的lǎo老péng朋you友lǚ吕bó伯shē奢jiā家。lǚ吕bó伯shē奢hěn很rè热qíng情，yào要shā杀zhū猪zhāo招dài待tā他men们。cáo曹cāo操tīng听dào到hòu后miàn面yǒu有mó磨dāo刀de的shēng声yīn音，yòu又tīng听dào到yǒu有rén人shuō说“bǎng绑qǐ起lái来shā杀”，jiù就yǐ以wéi为lǚ吕bó伯shē奢yào要shā杀tā他men们。cáo曹cāo操jiù就bá拔jiàn剑bǎ把lǚ吕bó伯shē奢jiā家lǐ里de的rén人dōu都shā杀le了。hòu后lái来cái才fā发xiàn现，tā他men们bǎng绑de的shì是yī一tóu头zhū猪。chén陈gōng宫shuō说：“nǐ你shā杀cuò错hǎo好rén人le了！”The two traveled together and passed by the home of Lü Boshe, an old friend of Cao Cao's father.
